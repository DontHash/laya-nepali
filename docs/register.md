## Register rules (generator prompt + judge rubric)

1. Keep messages short: reviewed commands sit at p50 6 words / p90 10; conversational speech runs p50 12 / p90 18.
2. Write the way people type on WhatsApp, not formal prose.
3. Use spoken particles where they fit: नि, है, त, ला, and दाइ/दिदी/हजुर for people (reviewed commands use them ~7 per 100 words).
4. Use English loanwords only where people borrow them (order, delivery, cart, rate): conversational podcasts run ~17% Latin tokens, but short command messages are often pure Devanagari.
5. Questions use कति/के/कहाँ/कहिले with verb-final phrasing.
6. Devanagari digits (१२३) are acceptable for quantities.
7. No emojis, no URLs, no names, addresses or phone numbers.

## Method

- Generated by `python -m layanep.reference --source <label=path> ... --out docs/register.md`.
- Lanes per `docs/provenance.md`: NepTrans/LINCE/tweets are reference-only; the reviewed batch is our own CC BY 4.0 data.
- Message-length guidance leans on the reviewed batch; particle and code-switch densities lean on the conversational references.
